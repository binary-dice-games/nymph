nymph charts

Pin the button next to the address bar. Open the puzzle-piece menu in Chrome's toolbar and click the pin beside "nymph charts".

The button shows whether nymph is on for the site you are reading and how many charts it found. Click it to turn nymph off or on for that site.

nymph turns nymph chart images (PNGs that carry their YAML source) into live charts you can pan, zoom and hover, on every site. Other images are left alone, and nothing you read leaves your browser.
