nymph charts

nymph needs access to the sites you read. Your browser has not granted it yet.

Pin the button next to the address bar. Open the puzzle-piece menu in the browser's toolbar and click the pin beside "nymph charts".

The button shows whether nymph is on for the site you are reading and how many charts it found. Click it to turn nymph off or on for that site.

nymph turns nymph chart images (PNGs that carry their YAML source) into live charts you can pan, zoom and hover, on every site. Other images are left alone, and nothing you read leaves your browser.

On the sites below, nymph reads the code blocks of a chat's answers. A block holding a nymph source is drawn as a live chart, with its code one click away. Nothing leaves your browser, and nymph never types into a chat or sends anything for you.

    Teach Gemini to draw nymph charts

    Gemini does not know nymph's format. Give it these instructions once, in a Gem:

    1. Open Gemini's new Gem page.
    2. Name the Gem "nymph", press the button below, and paste into its Instructions field.
    3. Save it, then ask that Gem for a chart.

    Or, in a chat already open, right-click nymph's toolbar button and choose "Give this chat the nymph instructions": they go into the prompt box and you send them yourself.
